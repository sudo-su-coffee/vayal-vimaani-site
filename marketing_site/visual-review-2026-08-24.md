# Vayal Vimaani visual review — Field Ceremony redesign

## Mobile capture
At 390 × 844px, the hero now reads as a calmer editorial composition with serif display type, rice-paper navigation, vermilion action button, and a four-column highlight strip. The headline and body copy remain readable, the primary booking action is visible without scrolling, and the mobile navigation trigger remains clear. The highlight strip is dense but still fits the viewport; follow-up should confirm the lower sections at full-page mobile height.

## Desktop capture
At 1440 × 900px, the hero has a stronger premium identity: deep indigo/tea green field treatment, large serif headline, vermilion CTA with offset gold shadow, and generous negative space. The drone image remains the dominant proof asset. The composition is distinctive without adding unfamiliar decorative symbols or reducing the clarity of the booking action.

## Design decision
Keep the Japanese inspiration structural and material—editorial typography, rice-paper surfaces, fine rules, numbered field notes, and restrained action color—while preserving Tamil Nadu language, farmer-first copy, direct phone/booking paths, and accessible mobile behavior.
