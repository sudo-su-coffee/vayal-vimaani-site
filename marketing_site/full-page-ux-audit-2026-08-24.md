# Vayal Vimaani full-page UX/UI audit — 2026-08-24

## Mobile (390 × 844)
The hero is visually strong and the primary booking action is visible. The mobile browser preview bar reduces the visible viewport, so the stats strip is partially clipped in the supplied screenshot; this is browser chrome rather than page overflow. The main issue is the booking section’s left content: it has a large heading, explanatory paragraph, and three contact links before the form, which makes the user scroll through too much supporting content before reaching the first input. The form itself is clear but begins below a large amount of content.

## Desktop (1440 × 900)
The hero has good visual hierarchy and a clear primary action. Navigation is compact and the image provides immediate agricultural context. The desktop booking section can support a stronger two-part composition by treating contact options as a compact action rail rather than a loose list, allowing the form to become the dominant conversion surface.

## High-impact changes to apply
Reduce the booking intro copy to a concise promise, group the direct contact methods into compact action chips, and make the form header carry more of the instructional load. Preserve direct phone and WhatsApp access, but do not let those links compete with the form. Maintain 48px controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, and one-column mobile stacking.
