# Reference comparison — desktop

The uploaded reference desktop page has a strong conversion-first hero. It uses one large drone-in-field image, a short direct headline, a government-supported badge, and two immediately visible actions: the Google Play badge and Call Expert. The collaboration strip beneath the hero adds institutional credibility early.

The current Vayal desktop page has a more distinctive editorial identity and substantially richer farmer-specific content. It covers field support, subsidy guidance, the app handoff, booking flow, and support actions more carefully. Its initial seasonal modal, however, hides the hero until dismissed and can make the first impression feel campaign-led rather than service-led.

## Useful content to borrow from the reference

The strongest reusable ideas are the direct “Book in app” hero action, an institutional collaboration strip using only approved logos, the six-service advantage section, the simple app workflow, and a practical “Join as Pilot” pathway if that service is still offered. The testimonials should not be copied as content unless they are verified first-party customer statements with permission.

## Useful image directions

The reference's most useful image direction is a real-looking drone operating over a recognizable crop field with enough dark negative space for readable copy. Supporting images worth recreating or sourcing are: drone spraying at working height, farmer and pilot discussing a field, a land-registration/map moment, a seasonal harvest visual, and a phone-based booking/support moment. The current site already has versions of most of these, but the hero and popup should avoid competing as simultaneous dominant visuals.

## Recommendation

Keep the current site's farmer-first content and accessible support model. Borrow the reference's simpler first-viewport conversion pattern: one direct app/support action, one secondary action, and a proof strip. Consider changing the seasonal offer popup from automatic opening on every visit to a dismissible announcement or delayed campaign banner, so first-time visitors see the actual service hero immediately.
