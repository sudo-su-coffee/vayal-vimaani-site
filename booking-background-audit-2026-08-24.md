# Booking background audit

The final booking section uses the village-field image as a continuous CSS background with a dark green contrast wash.

At the desktop preview width, the left field message is rendered as a cream floating panel approximately 611px wide, and the right support area contains a matched cream panel approximately 526px wide. Both have coordinated deep-green offset shadows. The left panel receives a gold hanging line and bracket from the upper-left; the support panel receives the matching hanger on its upper-right.

The booking section itself spans approximately 1265×857px, while the background image remains owned by the section rather than the inner caption card. Mobile rules collapse the two panels into one column and hide the hanger hardware for a cleaner narrow-screen layout.
